Optional NVIDIA runtime

The optional whisper.cpp CUDA archive contains NVIDIA proprietary runtime libraries. These are not covered by the whisper.cpp MIT license. The installer downloads the original archive from the upstream release.

CUDA 11.8 license terms:
https://docs.nvidia.com/cuda/archive/11.8.0/eula/index.html

Upstream release:
https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp/releases/tag/v1.9.1

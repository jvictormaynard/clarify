Granite Speech 5.0 TurboCTC 470M — GGUF conversions
https://huggingface.co/vladers/granite-speech-5.0-470m-turboctc-GGUF

The weights in this repository are IBM's granite-speech-5.0-470m-turboctc
(https://huggingface.co/ibm-granite/granite-speech-5.0-470m-turboctc), revision
1dd23f46f6d4e6eae2531982ba2e33220086dd97, which IBM releases under the Apache License,
Version 2.0 (see LICENSE). They are IBM's work.

Changes made to them in this repository:
- Converted from safetensors to GGUF with transcribe.cpp's converter. Tensors are renamed
  to transcribe.cpp's layout, each block's k_proj and v_proj are concatenated into one
  attn.kv tensor, the BatchNorm num_batches_tracked training counters are dropped, and a
  precomputed mel filterbank and Hann window are added.
- Stored as F32 (IBM's values exactly), BF16 (linear weights exactly; convolution
  kernels in F16), F16, or quantized to Q8_0, Q6_K, Q5_K_M or Q4_K_M.

Tools used to make them; none is redistributed here except the two patches:
- transcribe.cpp, https://github.com/handy-computer/transcribe.cpp, release 0.4.1
  (4d7c1ccefd39e5a9ea36cca5f8fefc6dcc14b99b). MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 The
  transcribe.cpp authors.
- conversion/granite5_ctc_multilingual.patch, by 17slever17,
  https://huggingface.co/17slever17/granite-speech-5.0-470m-turboctc-multilingual-GGUF,
  Apache License 2.0. Line endings normalized to LF; otherwise unchanged.
- conversion/granite5_ctc_deterministic_frontend.patch, written for this repository and
  offered under transcribe.cpp's MIT License so it can be upstreamed.
